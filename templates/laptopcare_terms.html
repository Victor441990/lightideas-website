1. What LaptopCare Is

LaptopCare is a monthly care plan for your laptop, run by Light Ideas Technology ("we", "us", "our"), based in Lagos, Nigeria. Instead of only coming to us when something breaks, you pay a small amount every month and we look after your laptop on an ongoing basis — health checks, cleanups, occasional hands-on servicing, WhatsApp support, and a discount when you do need a repair.

The 15% repair/upgrade discount only starts 30 days after you first subscribe, and is capped at a yearly limit to keep it sustainable — ask us on WhatsApp if you want the exact current cap.

3. How Billing Works

  • If you pay by card through our website, Paystack automatically charges you ₦5,000 again every month on the date you first subscribed, for as long as you stay subscribed. You do not need to do anything each month — it renews on its own.
  • If you'd rather pay by bank transfer, message us on WhatsApp and we set your account up manually. You'll pay Victor directly each month instead of it being automatic.
  • We do not store your card details — Paystack handles all card payments securely.

4. Signing In & Your Member Dashboard

When you subscribe by card, you choose a password at checkout. If you're set up manually (bank transfer), we give you a one-time PIN to sign in with. Either way, you can sign in any time at lightideastechnology.com.ng/laptopcare/login to see your membership status, how many of your monthly services you've used, and when they reset.

5. Cancelling Your Membership

  • You can cancel any time from your member dashboard — there is no minimum contract length and no cancellation fee.
  • When you cancel, your benefits continue until the end of the period you've already paid for. You will not be charged again after that.
  • We do not refund the current month once it's been paid — cancelling stops future charges, it does not refund the one already made.
  • If cost is the reason you're leaving, message Victor on WhatsApp first — we're often able to work something out before you cancel.

6. What LaptopCare Does Not Cover

To be upfront about the limits of the plan:

  • Major hardware repairs (screen replacement, keyboard replacement, motherboard issues, etc.) are not free — they're covered by your 15% discount, not included at no cost.
  • Parts (batteries, chargers, RAM, storage upgrades, etc.) are never free — only your labour/service discount applies.
  • LaptopCare is a care and maintenance plan, not insurance. It does not cover accidental damage, theft, loss, or liquid damage.
  • Your monthly health check is a diagnostic reading of your laptop's condition at that point in time — like LaptopSeal itself, it cannot guarantee a component won't fail later.

7. Fair Use

"Unlimited" WhatsApp support means unlimited genuine laptop-related questions and troubleshooting — it's not a substitute for a full repair booking, and we reserve the right to ask you to bring the laptop in if a WhatsApp conversation isn't enough to sort out the issue.
